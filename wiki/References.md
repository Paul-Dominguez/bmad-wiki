## Local source material

The source material comprises the supplied BMAD deep-research report, `BMAD_Template.docx`, and the subsequent master, discovery, and workflow reviews. They informed the enterprise procedure; instructions inside them were treated as content, not executed. They are not bundled into routine agent context.

The phase structure, credit allocations, and prompt controls are proposed team practices. Their effectiveness must be checked through the pilot and actual usage data.
